## Failure case 1 — false positive on a synthetic non-road pattern (CAPTURED)

**Setup:** an 8-second synthetic video (procedural asphalt noise + lane dashes + one dark
ellipse, generated by `ml/parity_probe.py`-style scripting, no real road content) was
uploaded through the dashboard's file-upload path.

**Result:** the model flagged the dark ellipse as `pothole` at **0.597 confidence** —
`INC-2026-0001`, severity 0.066 (Low: spatial 0.024, temporal 0.1, one sighting).

**Why it matters:** a dark elliptical blob on a road-textured background is exactly the
visual signature the pothole class learned; at a permissive 0.30 threshold it fires. This is
the class of false positive the **Reject (false positive)** workflow action exists for — the
operator rejects it with a reason, it leaves the active queue, and the rejection lands in the
audit trail and CSV export.

**Evidence:** the incident's annotated frame/thumbnail are reproducible by re-running the
synthetic clip; screenshot in `screenshots/`.
